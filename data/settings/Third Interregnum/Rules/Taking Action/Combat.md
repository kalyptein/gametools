## Recovery

Recovering HP
* When recovering HP, Grit is refreshed first, then Guard.  If you are over your Redline on Stress, you can't recover Grit.
* Every 10 minutes of low-stress activity, gain 1 Stress and recover 1d6 HP (+2 w/ the Hard to Kill edge).
	* If the amount recovered is greater than was needed, the recovery does not cost Stress.
	* If gaining Stress would take you above your Health, you do not gain it or recover HP.

Recovering Armor
* After 10 minutes out of combat, recover all armor points.

First Aid
* Requires a medical kit and 10 minutes of work.
* You can provide the equivalent of (Medicine) stress worth of extra healing to a person per Long Rest. (or reduce stress already incurred?)
	* Multiple medics can provide this, but are limited by their individual skill.  E.g. a Medicine-1 medic could provide 1 stress worth of healing to someone, then a Medicine-2 expert could provide another 1 stress.  If the Medicine-2 expert gets there first, they can provide 2 stress of healing, but the Medicine-1 medic would not be able to do any further good.

Resting
* Brief Rest
	* 10 minutes (1 scene) of low-stress activity.
	* Refreshes Brief Rest / Scene abilities.
* Short Rest
	* A few hours of low-stress activity in a safe place, with at least a little food and drink.
	* Refreshes Short Rest abilities.
* Long Rest
	* 8 hours of sleep in a safe place, having had adequate food and drink.
	* Remove 1 Stress (2 if in particularly relaxed and luxurious comfort).
	* Recover (Level) HP (may recover Grit regardless of current Stress total).
	* Refreshes Long Rest abilities.
