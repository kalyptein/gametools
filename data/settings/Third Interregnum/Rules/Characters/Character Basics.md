## Derived Stats
---

* HP: 
	* Total = (3.5 + CON) x Level, rounding up (up to 10th)
		* Hard to Kill edge: +2 per level
		* Above 10th: +1 per level (+2 with Hard to Kill edge)
	* Divide evenly into Guard and Grit pools (place any odd point into Guard)
* Health = 9 + (CON x 4) + (Athletics ranks)
	* Maximum possible Stress (from cyberware, drugs, and HP recovery).
	* Metabolic Load: total of the permanent Stress due to your cyberware.  Your Stress cannot be reduced below this level without removing cyberware.
	* Redline = Metabolic Load + ((Health - Metabolic Load) / 2), rounded down.
		* If you have Stress above your Redline, you cannot recover Grit, only Guard, through most means.
* ???Stability = 9  + ((AWA | CHA) x 4) + (Presence ranks)
	* MES?
* Base Attack Bonus: +(level / 2), rounded down
    - On Target edge: +(level)
* Survivor Bonus: +1 add to all resistance skill checks at 6th.
	* Base DIF 8 for resistances.

Attack
* base attack bonus = (level / 2), rounding down
	* = level (warriors)
* attack roll = 1d20 + base attack bonus + MOD + attack skill rating
	* melee attack while prone: -4
	* shooting from a prepared position (elevated, good field of fire): +2
	* thrown or pistol in melee: -4 (2h guns are impossible)
* 

Defense
* base defense: 12 + DEX + Survivor bonus
	* no combat experience at all: -2
	* warrior: +2
	* partial warrior: +1
	* light armor: +1 (warpaint, armored undersuit, secure clothing, hard vacc suit)
	* medium armor: +2 (security armor)
	* heavy armor: +3 (combat field uniform)
	* superheavy armor: +4 (assault suit)
* cover and movement
	* light concealment / thin cover (corner of building, car door, thin flipped table) +2
	* heavy concealment / firm cover (brick wall, sandbag barrier, foxhole) +4
	* solid cover (bunker, firing slit) +6
	* sprinting / broken ground running (must significantly lateral to incoming fire): +2
	* prone: +2 (distant), -2 (close)
* armor needs repairs as it gets shot up
	* 1 tick per serious combat, after some ticks it starts degrading, needs repair



### Inventory

* Unencumbered
	* Readied Items: 4 + DEX + Agility ranks
	* Carrying Capacity: 8 + (STR x 2) + Athletics ranks
* Encumbered
	* -1 to Agility, Athletics, and defense checks
	* 70% move speed
	* +2 Readied Items or +4 Stowed Items
* Heavily Encumbered
	* -2 to Agility, Athletics, and defense checks
	* 50% move speed
	* +4 Readied Items or +8 Stowed Items
