### Non-Hominins

- in some cases the 1st imperium xeno de-extinctions and xeno-hybrids made use of non-human terrestrial organisms, so there are xeno-dolphins or xeno-hedgehogs or whatever


??? swarm
* celocrae-like "swarm-in-a-trenchcoat" species
- engineered during the Diaspora from a terrestrial, eusocial insect swarm into a collectively dog-smart utility swarm used in terraforming and bottled ecologies aboard ships, stations, etc.
	- based on: ants?  could call them myrmidons?  bee? apisophonts?
- during the 1st imperium, exposure to the Choir-song uplifted some of them into collective sapience.
	- The swarms derived from that event continue as sapients, but it hasn't been duplicated
	- They reproduce by splitting and isolating long enough to stabilize into independent entities
	- boundaries between individuals are blurry, tine-like
		- two swarms can permanently exchange members, both to maintain genetic diversity and to share "selves" to a degree.  their memories and identities are partly biological, but mostly metadimensional (but still tied to the physical units of the swarm), mixing members allows some bleed between minds.
	- when they spread out too much, it's hard to think (again, very Tine-like), reaction time and cognition worsen.  destruction of a significant amount of the swarm can render them non-sapient (though there are stories of badly reduced swarms eventually rebuilding and reintegrating)
	- they occupy and collectively puppeteer shells, sometimes humanoid, to act in human manner

### Heraldics

* animals engineered by 2nd Imperium noble houses as combination living heraldic beasts (lionoids, wolfoids, eagloids, etc) and house retainers.
* ??? They aren't uplifted, but are cyberware brains implanted in living bodies, loaded with an integral (non-transferable) VI.  The VI in many is human-level, but scratch-built, rather than just a human emulation; often incorporating real or idealized / metaphorical qualities of the animal
* They were often given excellent memories and used as house geneaologists / historians, able to recite the deeds of the family.  Many are predators, joining festive hunts and able to act as bodyguards (keen sense of smell to detect poison or explosives).  They could serve as heralds or major-domos.  Some were trained in strategy and espionage and used as trusted confidants.
* Most were given manipulatory appendages so they could use human tech, though some were left as purely decorative beasts (and most of these have died out).
