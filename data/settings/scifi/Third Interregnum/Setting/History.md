## Third Imperium

* merchant princes / plutocracy (fell 200-ish years ago)
	* gradually formed as far-trader houses rebuilt from the ashes of the 2nd empire, beginning to consolidate into skirmishing rival cartels, which eventually had it out in a series of Consolidation Wars that destroyed or absorbed the losers and left a single dominant alliance of houses to become the new empire.
* more of a rulership between the stars, over trade routes, economics, ship building, etc.
	* civilzation gradually recovered from the violent fall of the 2nd empire, trade and travel rebuilt.  a group of far-traders achieved breakout success, consolidating and monopolizing much of space travel and its services (shipping, trade, communications).  rather than directly try to conquer worlds, they would threaten blockade, privateering, destroying satellites, etc, crippling a world's economy unless they bent the knee and joined the empire.
* as long as subject planets stuck with the program (the local satraps kept in power by imperium backing) and the trade flowed, the empire was less concerned with imposing cultural / social structures
* some family-corporation-houses have survived the fall to one degree or another.
* some of the Bonded Companies, the mercenary legions of the empire, have survived in one form or another, giving rise to warlords, pirate fleets, and still-extant elite mercenary companies.
	* rose to prominence in the run-up to and main event of the Consolidation Wars.
* Interac - current lingua franca, used by the 3rd empire
* The empire collapsed due to an unprecedented mega-storm event that disrupted FTL travel for a generation.
	* Imperial ships were trapped in many systems, forced to submit to the groundside forces.  Trade routes collapsed, industries were starved.  Satraps were overthrown or planets plunged into civil wars.  Lots of derelict stations and ships around, stranded in empty systems or damaged mid-jump.  Ship-building, navigation, etc had to be rebuilt from scratch on many worlds, after imperial monopoly had crushed any local industry.
