Prophet of the Final Day

* mapper of fate, navigator toward doom and the end of all things, master of all-devouring time, watcher in / of the wasteland, patron of silence and stillness
	* dealable, not generally hostile, content to wait
* somehow comes from the future?) offeror of prophecy (it always, on the grandest scale, leads toward the End, even if not in the moment)
	* he has a Broken Wing Crane-like book; he is writing it and when it is done, it will be the end of days.  but also sometimes he can read passages he hasn't written yet, and so gain knowledge of the future
* one of the oldest ghul gods, a tutor / inspirer of the others, originator of the ghul way of delight and scholarship in entropy
	* he began his long walk in the elder age
	* he was actually deified before the ghul ever took physical form; one title might be something like "The Unformed / Unembodied / Bodiless / He Who Is Without Form"
	* 
	* might he actually be an Oracle?
* 
* dominions: time, fate, knowledge, death

The Unhallower
* a master of undead, tomb-maker, gourmet of cannibalism, god of vampires, tutor of liches, concierge of mummies, anti-psychopomp, inverter of the cycle of life and death, grand professor of necromancy
* he basically invented undeath, extrapolating from the nature of ghul as hungry spirts within vessels
* he showed the way when the ghul first took forms, having begun his study when they still lurked in the depths
	* he was deified in the elder age for this trailblazing on behalf of the ghul
* 
* dominions: death, life?



Labyrinth Maker
* a craftsman of nightmares, madness, and labyrinths, crafter of puzzleboxes, thief, collector, and hoarder of the treasures of the past
* 
* ??? deified through his work in the age of night? (creating nightmares)
	* might also have returned in the age of twilight, perhaps alongside the Pathogenitor? and had a similar arc, molesting the fey, intrigued by their weird time and malleable reality
* 
* dominions: dream, madness, knowledge, trickery, travel?


Pathogenitrix

* artist of decay, poison, and disease; gardener of poisonous plants and beasts, designer of torture devices, forger of cruel blades and barbs, cultivator of suffering
* 
* deified in the age of twilight?  one of the few ghul to return to the fulcrum world
	* perhaps brough back, or followed back, dwarven underworld explorers?
	* brewed many misfortunes for the fey, a well known "devil-like" Adversary that fey heroes struggled against
		* he could do some horrible shit to malleable gossamer biology
	* maybe enthralled cultists among the dwarves, drawing them into fascination as they studied ghul artifacts and lore
* 
* dominions: pain, nature, creation? life? death?

The Twin Dramaturges

* dramaturge of strife, conductor of betrayal and conspiracy, wheels within wheels, breaker of ideals, hopes, and beliefs, the mockery, twister of language
	* twin gods of this? or brother/sister?
* they made their bodies out of the same pair of malakim/djinn
	* engineered some kind of apple of discord scenario that unfolded into some really satisfyingly epic strife
* the are the warmains of the ghul, having an interest in battle more from a dramatic standpoint, but you need to be good at warfare to ensure you do a lot of harm
	* they were deified for their glorious work in the Elder War
* 
* dominions: war, trickery, art, desire

Harbinger of the Turning Age

* actual psychopomp, patron of endings, giver of mercy, shedder of skins, the dross-consuming-flame, lord of the turning age, maker of ways for new things, sower of ashes to enrich the soil
	* shiva-like, actually benevolent in many ways; thus on the outs with the other ghul some of the time, but not always
* a deadly warrior; some might worship him for his aspect of prowess, rather than his clearing of the way
* demigod for his prowess in the Elder War, but deified for his part in the end of the age of night and his mercybringing during that terrible time; unlike the other ghul gods, he had many devotees among the mortals
* 
* dominions: death, war, storm/disaster
