
## Oozes

- some are Chaos-y, extrusions of primordial ooze, vital and charged with elemental energy (fire, cold, etc).
- some are Entropic, products of decay bent on consuming and dissolving, or the residue of curdled thought that congeals in places of trauma and madness.

## Genius Locii ???

- spirits, usually with a great god bent, which shape and influence a place, group, etc. some might wander, inflicting their purpose/vision upon the world
    - chaotic and generative (elementals, totemic beasts), lawful and shaping (inevitables, lares, fate-weavers), entropic and eroding (plaguebringers, corpse-collectors, sowers of misfortune and strife)

- malakim made "patterns spiders", spirits to shape the world and guide its order. still exist, out shaping the world and fate, imposing on people, or perhaps allied with a Gathering and seeking to uphold its structure. Candidates for genius locii.
