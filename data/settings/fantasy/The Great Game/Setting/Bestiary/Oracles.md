
## The Glatisant (The Questing Beast)

* Appeared in the Age of Twilight, pursued by many, especially among the Wild Hunt, but never bagged.
	* There are a few relic weapons and other items that have made the pursuit and come close to the goal, and bear some power from it.
		* A few arrows that have tasted the Glatisant's blood.  Horsehoes or boots that have long trodden its path.  Cloaks that endured the pursuit through torrential rain or biting cold.  Spyglasses, cunning maps, etc
* It knows much.  Seeking it is always a quest and ordeal, often long, sometimes fruitless.
	* It leads pursuers into trials, traps, tribulations, enemies, and threats, but also opportunities to learn, grow, or gain.  It seems to enjoy the pageantry, the pursuer's strivings, and the drama.  Sometimes it even battles the pursuer in person.
	* When it finally deigns to be caught / run to ground, it offers cryptic advice, directs one on to new quests to achieve goals, find things, shift cruxes, etc.  It always seems to be able to slip away, even if seemed to have been cornered / captured / sorely wounded.
* It can follow paths that let it cross the world swiftly and in secret; pursuers may find themselves unexpectedly drawn to far off lands, or arriving after surprisingly little or long spans of time.  It may also allow a pursuer to use it to travel in this manner, paying the toll for travel via their pursuit.
* The Glatisant has shown up in many, many places across the world, though it does seem to have some preferred stomping grounds.
