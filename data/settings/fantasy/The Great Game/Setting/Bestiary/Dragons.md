## Dragons

- The first dragons were born from the destructive forces unleashed in the war of the ur-races, fused into elemental matter. Later dragons have been born from newer upheavals, such as the end of the Age of Twilight.
    - dragons "breed" by sowing devastation in a new area and lairing their for a time, raising a clutch of elemental "eggs" which hatch into wyrmlings. The dragon abandons the area after they're established, returning to their primary lair or going on to new devastation. The wyrmlings usually compete, killing and devouring each other unless the desolation is large enough to support many (some might survive and migrate into other lands).
- They have aspects of chaos and entropy, born from the fusion of misery and suffering with elemental power. The wild's eternal now does not provide so much suffering and it repairs and renews itself readily, so it is the destruction of the lands and lives of sapient beings that is most potent in breeding and feeding dragons.
- They covet and hoard the things made by sapients, particularly the treasures of civilized ones of Law, as well as claiming their lands for the dragon's lair and surrounding Desolation. The deprivation satisfies them and breeds further suffering, and it tempts would-be heroes to them.

- Some mortal players of kismet hunt the dragons for their ancient kismet power and to protect the world; draconic power readily infuses into other things, since that's how dragons come to be; thus it can be taken by a hunter, but also spill onto someone by chance. this is why draconic bone, blood, etc is so valuable/powerful in crafting magical things or empowering enchantments

- Some dragons fall fully to Entropy, lairing down in the Underworld and becoming corrupted (eating undead and demons can do that too). Often these are survivors driven from their surface desolations by rival dragons or heroes, exiles and survivors in the endless dark.
- Are there any "good" or "benevolent" dragons? Maybe some few have turned to Law after brooding upon their hoarded works of civilization and having an epiphany of their value and significance. Or somehow developing empathy of the folk whose sorrow birthed them.
- Some might turn lawful after spending long enough extorting nearby settlements that they come to appreciate the life of a lawful tyrant, dispensing iron law and being served; having Law make and bring you monuments and tribute also scratches the "hoarding" and "depriving folk" itches.

  

- The Elder Dragons were born from the churning elements of the battlefield Fulcrum World and the suffering of the ur-races as they warred.
- The already weary ur-races gave way, retreating back out to their edges of the world as the Elder Dragons spread and claimed desolations, often taking now-abandoned fortresses, palaces, and the like.
- The Fulcrum World, cleared of partisan powers, settled and stabilized. The presence of the dragons kept intrusive powers from the world.
- Eventually, the dragons slumbered. Without their dominion, the world was left empty of power, in a balanced, calm, plastic state that gave rise to the Age of Twilight.
- The elder dragons are still slumbering? Probably drawn deep into the Underworld by now, or shifted into the Ascended Realm? Might be the subject of cults, sorcerous-prodding, etc. Probably apocalyptic disaster if one woke.
- Did the elder dragons contribute to a Dominion? Their own? Another?
- The New Dragons of the Age of Night were born from eggs left by the elders, hatched by the new burst of destruction and suffering as the Wode fell, Night claimed the world, and the Fires-in-the-Night ignited.
