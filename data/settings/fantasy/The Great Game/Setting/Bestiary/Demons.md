- "demonic": a broken language made from the dross of all other languages, the language of madness and dying thoughts stitched back together. to hear it is degrading to sanity, to speak if tears at the soul, but it is undeniably powerful, a "bile" of the mind and spirit, used to melt and fuse things. instinctively understood by all demons, even if they can't speak yet.


- In the depths of the Underworld, around the mouth of the Abyss, a great slurry of dissolving reality oozes.
- From this decaying but still fecund and volatile mix, demons are born; nightmare amalgams for form, life, incoherent mad thought and emotion, hungry for substance and order. As they successfully feed, they grow more complex and cobble together a kind of alien mind, reaching upward to consume every more bountiful feasts.
    - the first demons sprang from the bubbling froth of shed divine blood and torn flesh and raw power from the first great clash between the ur-races in kismet, brewing up in the early part of the Age of Twilight.

- Demons are independent and composed of all 3 (4?) Great Gods: the hunger and detritus of entropy, the seething generative power and mutability of form of chaos, and the teleological growth toward complexity of law. Some sages suggest this makes them one of Kismet's plays; a less harmonious one to her other children then usual. Others see them as an accident, inimical to all reality, a cancer of existence.

- many demons have a degree of shapeshifting, either a particular stolen and melded form, the ability to steal skins/forms, adaptation to new environments/conditions, or full-on shapeshifting

- "bile" or "demon bile" is a caustic fluid that acts as the digestive acid of the Abyss, the liquid solvent in the vast demonic flesh-swamp that breaks down and allows recombination of matter, thought, and spirit.
    - all demons have bile within them, to digest and absorb new material into themselves
    - if bile gets loose, and conditions are right, in an environment with a lot of "spare parts" (abattoir, battlefield, hospital, place of chaotic power, etc), it can begin to digest it an give spontaneous rise to demons or demonic upheavals (surging chaotic disasters without a sense of self, just a catastrophe unleashed)
  

- there are some demons who actually evolved into "wholesome" forms, leaving their original nature largely behind. others are beings with complex agendas, but still broken and predatory.

### The Hive Queen

## Taxonomy of Common Demons

### Ogres

- ogres are a class of demi-demon, oni-like. cannibalistic, gleefully brutal and predatory, but sapient. they can keep slaves and make deals. they often wield a bit of magic: shapechanging, enslaving animals, belching miasma, sending evil dreams, smelling hidden things, etc. they vary widely in appearance; might have extra limbs, spines, armor, often horns and tusks, talons, tails, etc.
