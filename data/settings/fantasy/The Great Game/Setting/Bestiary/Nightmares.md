## Nightmares

- (Aberrations) (beholders "olothec", illithid, etc)
- also part of the entropic world, creatures of madness and curdled dreams (of mind, but also expressed in warped flesh).
- born from the madness / nightmares of a creature, like a parasite (and can infect others and make a new brood), especially from madness/stress tied to plays of kismet, or from those with a lot of power; the madness survives its host, or in some cases the host becomes the nightmare at the end, apotheosis into a monster.
- some are bred by the ghul torturing victims, the pain, bad hallucinogens, etc giving birth to the madness within them.
