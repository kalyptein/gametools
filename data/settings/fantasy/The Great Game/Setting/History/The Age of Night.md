* magma gardens (oases or rifts)
* volcanic rifts and mountains
* horrible demonic flesh jungles / swamps (and their dried / petrified husks), geysers, lakes, and cenotes of bile, vast thorn eruptions
* guarded pockets of Twilight, hidden fey fastnesses
* dwarf citadels (including some biodomes to preserve older lifeforms)
* ruined fey palaces, gardens, villages, etc
* ghul bastions


- time begins, becoming clearer, sharper; the sun and moon set; Entropy is ascendant
    - the stars still shine, but are often obscured by dark clouds of dust, soot, or worse
- the breach of the demons into the surface world is the epoch-turning event from Twilight. They had been festering in the Underworld through the prior age, but now erupt.
- Cosmological upheaval results in environmental upheaval (volcanos, earthquakes)
    - rise of New Dragons
- The Fires-in-the-Night burn
    - volcanic rifts of chaos, oases of heat, life, and dim red light in the darkness
    - Azhi? Titans? Behemoths? vitalizing kaiju dwell within, sustaining, their wrath protects the oases from the ghul and their progeny
    - orcs are birthed from the flame and the azhi-blood
- Night-Land-like world of endless darkness, hunger, monsters, breakdown of cosmic order
- the Wode begins to wither, hard desolate time overcoming its malleable luxury.
    - Wild Hunt now becomes more serious, bent on preserving the Wode (mostly unsuccessfully over the age). Drives back the things of the Night Age
    - Wode is broken, fading into pockets of dreamtime as the Age progresses.
- dragons ravage where they will. demons bubble to the surface and roam.

- the cities of fey, the holds of dwarves, are besieged by demons, undead, nightmares and the scattering of ghul who remain.
    - the underlands claimed by dwarves come under siege and contract or fall. Some surface citadels are overwhelmed from below, while others fortify and hold out. These lost dwarven lands are in the Underworld today, with their treasures.
    - ghul hunt for sport, besiege the bastions, engage in their decadent revels and mad experiments, unleashing horrors
- ancient ruins from the war and elder days now deep in the underworld, hanging above the abyss, treasures of seraph, djinn, elder days. dwarf-holds, fey ruins not far above.

- some of the more coherent / evolved demonlords and their broods create citadels and rule over cowed mortal populations of slaves / captives / toys / cultists / livestock.
	- More future ruins to explore!
	- Some might even still survive to the present, as do some of their works (magic items, cults, etc).

- some of the darker Divine Courts (death, etc), New Gods (undead, dwarves, elven heroes, orcs)
- The fire-oases of the Fires-in-the-Night spread, bringing life and warmth, consuming demonic hosts, burning away their flesh-portals to the surface and rekindling the celestial lights, ushering in the First Dawn.

  

- as the age of night peaked, the most developed demon lords were turning their eyes toward the ascendant realms and godhood.
	- They contested and intrigued and warred with one another, gathering the power to make the leap.
- the culmination of the age of night was the demon lords' great push for ascendance, and the rise of the dwarves, fey, and orcs against them, and ultimately the unleashing of the fires-in-the-night.
	- Some did ascend, but much was thwarted.
	- call it The War of Ascension? The Dawn War (as it literally ends with the First Dawn?)

  

- the age of night ended for a few crucial factors
    - when the Gates of Flesh on the surface were destroyed, and many of the demons were slain by the orcs and Flames in the Night.
	    - rather, the Gates of Flesh (a demonlord) ascended, but its earthly avatars were destroyed
- The new life flourishing above (and perhaps the returned sun and moon) gave the surface a degree of "immune system" against demonic corruption that it lacked initially.
	- The malleable substance of the Wode was particularly vulnerable to demonic absorption and corruption.
    - It's now harder for demons to proliferate / assimilate and open gates of flesh.


* ghul returned, though not en masse, intrigued by this age of entropy
	* they studied demons and all manner of other things
	* they didn't take a commanding role, being vastly outnumbered by the demons, who are not allies (occasionally of convenience), seeking growth, not decay
	* they were artists, scholars, scientists, and craftsmen of entropy.  many came to sift through the wreckage of fey civilization, others to hunt for sport among the mortals and demons
* the ones who came may ofte have been cults / devotees of the ascended ghul, perhaps hoping their patron could capitalize on the age of night and grow greater
