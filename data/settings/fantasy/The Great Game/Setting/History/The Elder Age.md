

## The Loom

- Kismet unveils the Loom of Souls
	- Malakim and Djinn are born
	- the ghul hungry spirits are born too, but are largely unknown, still unbodied
- elder civilizations flourish
	- the Scholar of the Last Day sets the ghul on the path of aesthetic appreciation of Entropy, is deified


## The Great War of the Elder Races

- Unbalancing of the Sun and Moon, Tides and Lunar Phases, Seasons
- Forces marshal at the Fulcrum
    - Djinn and Malakim clash
    - Ghul weave bodies from the slain, emerge and join the war.
	    - The Unhallower, inventor of undead, pioneers this art and is deified for it.
- Elder Dragons arise in the chaos
	- claim the battlefield of the Fulcrum World, driving out the weary, warring powers
- The Powers That Be are weary
	- First Sunset, sun and moon sink to the horizon
	- The surviving Elder Races retreat to their respective domains
	- the Fulcrum World rests in the aftermath of the war
	- Elder Gods arise from members of ascendant elder races, first Dominions form
