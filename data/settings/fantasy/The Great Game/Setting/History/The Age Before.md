
# Opening Moves

- Abyss, Horizon, and Heavens unfold

- In the first age, the Moon once belonged wholly to Law. In the second age, Chaos won a victory, and it now waxes and wanes.
    - It also tugs the seas into tides. Or perhaps the tides are Law's imposition on Chaos's sea???
    - Law at least managed to make it an orderly and predictable change. But sometimes there is a strange moon, out of order and with strange appearance and influence, when Chaos wins a play.
- Before the second age, the sun and moon stood in the sky eternally against the splendor of the stars and "milky way". Then Entropy won a victory and they are now dragged from the sky each night, and the cosmos is obscured by day. Sometimes it tries to devour the sun and moon in the underworld, before they can rise again.

* the fulcrum world forms at the center, still a churning cauldron of change and creation
