# Opening Moves

- Abyss, Horizon, and Heavens unfold

# The Heavens Unmarred

- Kismet unveils the Loom of Souls, Malakim and Djinn are born
- elder civilizations flourish

## The Great War of the Elder Races

- Unbalancing of the Sun and Moon, Tides and Lunar Phases, Seasons
- Forces marshal at the Fulcrum
    - Djinn and Malakim clash
    - Ghul weave bodies from the slain, emerge and join the war
    - Elder Dragons arise in the chaos, claiming the battlefield of the Fulcrum World, driving out the weary, warring powers
- First Sunset, sun and moon sink to the horizon
- The Powers That Be are weary
    - The surviving Elder Races retreat to their respective domains
    - the Fulcrum World rests in the aftermath of the war
    - Elder Gods arise from members of ascendant elder races, first Dominions form

# Age of Twilight

- the elder dragons slumber in their lairs, ceasing to dominate the world, leaving it still, empty, and unclaimed
- age of the fey, first born of the "mortals".
- "time" becomes uncertain/dreamlike, time serves no great god: fluid, unpredictable, undecaying
- The Dominion of the Wode flourishes
- Falling Stars (dwarves arrive), dig into/attack the underworld, but resist the fey-dream as well, but only in small pockets
- Ascendant archfey and new Dominions
- Wild Hunt hunt pursues the elder dragons and mythic beasts, a chivalric revel and contest

# Age of Night

- time begins, becoming clearer, sharper; the sun and moon set; Entropy is ascendant
    - the stars still shine, but are often obscured by dark clouds of dust, soot, or worse
- the breach of the demons into the surface world is the epoch-turning event from Twilight. They had been festering in the Underworld through the prior age, but now erupt.
- Cosmological upheaval results in environmental upheaval (volcanos, earthquakes)
    - rise of New Dragons
- The Fires-in-the-Night burn
    - volcanic rifts of chaos, oases of heat, life, and dim red light in the darkness
    - Azhi? Titans? Behemoths? vitalizing kaiju dwell within, sustaining, their wrath protects the oases from the ghul and their progeny
    - orcs are birthed from the flame and the azhi-blood
- Night-Land-like world of endless darkness, hunger, monsters, breakdown of cosmic order
- the Wode begins to wither, hard desolate time overcoming its malleable luxury.
    - Wild Hunt now becomes more serious, bent on preserving the Wode (mostly unsuccessfully over the age). Drives back the things of the Night Age
    - Wode is broken, fading into pockets of dreamtime as the Age progresses.
- dragons ravage where they will. demons bubble to the surface and roam.

- the cities of fey, the holds of dwarves, are besieged by demons, undead, nightmares and the scattering of ghul who remain.
    - the underlands claimed by dwarves come under siege and contract or fall. Some surface citadels are overwhelmed from below, while others fortify and hold out. These lost dwarven lands are in the Underworld today, with their treasures.
    - ghul hunt for sport, besiege the bastions, engage in their decadent revels and mad experiments, unleashing horrors
- ancient ruins from the war and elder days now deep in the underworld, hanging above the abyss, treasures of seraph, djinn, elder days. dwarf-holds, fey ruins not far above.

- some of the more coherent / evolved demonlords and their broods create citadels and rule over cowed mortal populations of slaves / captives / toys / cultists / livestock. More future ruins to explore! Some might even still survive to the present, as do some of their works (magic items, cults, etc).

- some of the darker Divine Courts (death, etc), New Gods (undead, dwarves, elven heroes, orcs)
- The fire-oases of the Fires-in-the-Night spread, bringing life and warmth, consuming demonic hosts, burning away their flesh-portals to the surface and rekindling the celestial lights, ushering in the First Dawn.

  

- as the age of night peaked, the most developed demon lords were turning their eyes toward the ascendant realms and godhood. They contested and intrigued and warred with one another, gathering the power to make the leap.
- the culmination of the age of night was the demon lords' great push for ascendance, and the rise of the dwarves, fey, and orcs against them, and ultimately the unleashing of the fires-in-the-night. Some did ascend, but much was thwarted. The War of Ascension? The Dawn War (as it literally ends with the First Dawn?)

  

- the age of night ended for a few crucial factors
    - when the Gates of Flesh on the surface were destroyed, and many of the demons were slain by the orcs and Flames in the Night.
- The new life flourishing above (and perhaps the returned sun and moon) gave the surface a degreee of "immune system" against demonic corruption that it lacked initially. The malleable substance of the Wode was particularly vulnerable to demonic absorption and corruption.
    - It's now harder for demons to proliferate / assimilate and open gates of flesh.

# First Dawn

- Sun and Moon rise, days turn
- the Fires-in-the-Night are spent, gradually cooling and calming, leaving rich ash, hot springs, and gently smoking calderas.
- savage life flourishes (green hells, hungry swamps, sargasso seas, trackless forests, great beasts)
    - orcs flourish
    - Dwarves hold their citadels, the eldest civilization of the age
    - pockets of Wode and fey-kind remain. The first wode-reclaimers begin to plot among the elves to regather fragments of the Wode and remake the world as it once was, when it was theirs.
- The coming of men (Gen and Gol), goblins (and similar)
- Djinn return and luxuriate in the wilderness, make villas, toy with primitive mortalkind, giving gifts, sporting, hunting, etc.
    - ??? Gen and her people (ironically?) learn civilized arts from the djinn, serving and worshipping, while Gol and hers remain nomadic and unbowed.
    - the "first kingdom of men" ruled by Gen is destroyed by Gol to "free them". The seeds of a hundred new kingdoms are sown.
    - Their taming of the wilds is the slow crux of the turning of the age. The wild fertility wanes, and world becomes more lawful.
	    - great beasts are slain or tamed or driven back, lands settled, plowed, and bound in roads; cities rise and withstand savage assault

- New Gods, new Divine Courts (from god-kings, great beasts, etc)
    - As the age wanes, most djinn withdraw back to the Horizon.

# ???

- God-kings of the new kingdoms carve out stability for civilization from foes and upheavals, cities are born. The unbridled wilderness wanes.

# Old Kingdoms

- the cities of old have grown vast, formed empires, heavy with history, law, and wealth, but now faltering. Wilderness and decay and internal issues creep in.
- The Rise of Hell to challenge the old orders





- In the first age, the Moon once belonged wholly to Law. In the second age, Chaos won a victory, and it now waxes and wanes.
    - It also tugs the seas into tides. Or perhaps the tides are Law's imposition on Chaos's sea???
    - Law at least managed to make it an orderly and predictable change. But sometimes there is a strange moon, out of order and with strange appearance and influence, when Chaos wins a play.
- Before the second age, the sun and moon stood in the sky eternally against the splendor of the stars and "milky way". Then Entropy won a victory and they are now dragged from the sky each night, and the cosmos is obscured by day. Sometimes it tries to devour the sun and moon in the underworld, before they can rise again.
