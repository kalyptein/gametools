
The lower air

* Use "Skycrawl" for gyres, travel, sites, "suns and moons"
* skyships don't work below a certain altitude, they rely on the magic of the heavens
	* require other magic to ascend and descend, or very tall mountains or towers (or tall but less insanely tall towers on mountains, perhaps with gantries lowered from skyships)
* lots of ruins and former outposts of the Malakim, and some of the sky-inclined djinn
* the lower air is composed of various gyres, with mostly-impassable turbulence between, with occasional currents linking them (kind of like Starforged sectors and the gulfs between)
