

## Dragonborn / Draconians

- origins
    - people of any ancestry who are caught up in the disaster when a dragon manifests from anguish and elemental power, or less often when a dragon brood is born in its desolation, the fertile power can infect a mortal as well (such as a captive, hiding survivor, or stalking dragon hunter).
    - some hunters of dragons consume draconic power (blood, flesh, etc; elden ring-style)
    - some savage hordes ally with dragons (gol, orcs, goblins, etc), gaining aid and drinking their power in shaman's brews. In return they tithe from their plunder, or leave the dragon to claim the ruins in their wake. Most who drink their brew show minor draconic mutations, but some are more fully transformed.
    - sometimes a brooding dragon's egg will hatch into a draconian, instead of a young wyrm; perhaps a contamination of mortality (perhaps corpses incorporated into the egg?)

- draconians don't have a uniform appearance; they just incorporate various draconic elements (scales, eyes, wings, claws, forked tongue, tail, etc) in various combinations, usually proportionate to how powerful the draconic essence in them is. Some are dragon-headed, some more like humans (or whatever) with significant touch of rept8lian features

- draconic alchemy (dragon hunting, disaster surviving, etc) (draconian/dragonborn)

## Gnolls / Beastmen

- animals (not just hyenas) infected with a demonic parasite that transforms them into humanoid forms and fills them with ravening hunger and cruelty and dark magic
- can infect other races too, turns them into wendigo-like cannibals who blend right in with the animal-gnolls

- product of a specific demon lord or other named demonic agent / force. It periodically seeds an area, and gnolls spring up like a locust plague, sudden and devastating, sweeping through the area until they burn out / are burned out. Some tend to survive, becoming persistent packs.
    - maybe the originator benefits from this? what they eat feeds it? or they sacrifice to it? or they try to summon it?


## Revenants

- deceased mortals brought back to life by enigmatic powers; some say they are direct plays by Law and Chaos. They are not hungry dead, aligned with Entropy, and need no sustenance.
    - Law: revenants with some unfulfilled purpose: a duty, obligation, or oath to uphold.
    - Chaos: revenants filled with overwhelming emotion or desire: vengeance, need to protect, love, etc.
- some human kingdoms are ruled by revenants; law working to keep these ancient bastions in place by any means?
