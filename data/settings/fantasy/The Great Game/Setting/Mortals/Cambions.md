The Seven Cities / the Alliance of Hell

- devils are evolved humans, shaped by their pursuit of naked power, dominance, and freedom to indulge their desires (Do As Thou Wilt Shall Be The Whole Of The Law), using their wins at Kismet's game to empower and transform. Seven Cities pursue the aim; rebellious or rogue devil lords lead nomadic packs beyond their domains, seeking to claim a piece or topple a city.
- Their lands are beginning to show the Mandate of Heaven as they try to spread their order across the world, transforming to match the rulers. The human nations are beset.
- Much of the population of the cities are human. It is a mark of status to be allowed / gift to begin transforming and evolving into diabolic form.
- Devils have free will, and there are those who abandon their homeland or struggle against it, or seek some expression of the Luciferian principle outside tyranny over others.

  

- Devils can be made from any mortal race (dwarf, elf, etc) though most are human-derived.
