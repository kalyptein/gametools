* reproduction
	* a crystal from one of the geode meteors (or somehow cultivated otherwise?) is pierced into a stone, which gradually transmutes into a humanoid form and then awakens as a dwarf
		* runes are carved all over it, Dwarves place their hands on the stone and speak to it, calling out the dwarf within it.  The community often gathers in the area to chant and play music and engage in community.
			* This imparts a kind of cultural / community memory to the new dwarf.  They must meditate and study to assimilate it all, but it is greatly accelerated over normal learning.
			* there is an element of "gifts of the fairy godmothers" to the new dwarf; the runes, the words, the wishes, etc are thought to shape the new dwarf's nature, personality, path in life, etc.
		* this process takes months, sometimes years, and draws vitality, chiefly from the ones who lay on hands and breathe upon the stone.
			* a solo dwarf is seldom able to give enough vitality (and quickly enough) to kindle the new dwarf.  A pair can, though its is exhausting.  Usually a handful of dwarves take on the task.
			* These lifegivers are seen as the parents, expected to raise / train / guide the new dwarf, the "inner kin", while the rest of the clan that offered community and a trace of vitality are "outer kin"
		* sometimes the stone transmuted (always carefully chosen) is sculpted somewhat before being awoken / kindled, but not too much is expected or required.
	* the initial impact of the meteors created the first crop of dwarves, born from stone

* biology
	* their flesh is fleshy, but also tough and somewhat stone-like
		* they have skin, coloration, and quirks resulting from the stone they are born from; marbling, metallic veins or sheens of skin, mineral inclusions, specklings of crystals and gems.
			* certain dwarves are born with gems in their flesh that can become channels for unique magics; especially if they replace an eye; a blessed feature that recalls the crystal eyes of the Malakim (sometimes a third eye bindi, or palm jewel eye)
			* veins of metal can also be a channel, the metal offering certain properties, often ascribed to shape the dwarf's personality too.
			* the runes incised during their creation sometimes remain on their flesh as well
	* dwarves have gender, a byproduct of the Loom of Souls and the lasting imprint of the djinn, but no reproductive features relating to it
	* they are heavy, being born of stone and metal
		* they can't swim well, and are uneasy on boats
		* they are very sturdy, hard to hurt and hard to shove around
	* dwarves are bit out of tune with the natural world, deriving from the heavens, arriving the gossamer reality of Twilight, and then dwelling in the Underworld.
		* they have an anti-affinity for dealing with plants and animals of the surface world, not an animosity, but a "clumsiness"
	* dwarves have crystalline hearts
		* from the hearts of the deceased, the slivers of crystal to create new dwarves are made

	- Dwarves grow more stony / crystalline in their old age.  Upon death, dwarves fossilize (return to stone).


- dwarven "reality" is a bit more "solid" than everyon else's. They are more resilient to magic, particularly magic that alters the body, mind, or spirit. They are especially resilient to glamour.
