## Dwarves

- Elemental-infused beings???. They have opted to dwell in the Underworld, harvesting its elements to craft and carving order into its primal churning. This has kept them out of direct contention for the Mandate of Heaven, as they live outside the bounds of the heart of reality.
- They craft "keystones" or "hearts" for the domains, dwarf-made genius loci to give order to the local region.

- Sometimes dwarves have fallen under the sway of other powers. Morbid obsessives of decay and death, in thrall to Entropy, or its undead agents (perhaps entombed dwarf lords unwilling to not take it all with them). Others may fall to Chaos, maddened and enthralled by the flow of the elements, abandoning order to revel in unleashing the most violent transformations upon the world they can (volcanos, earthquakes, collapses, etc). Some may bow to exiled gods or other strange beings of the below.

- dwarves are, as a general rule, strongly aligned with Law. They are born from geode meteors that fall from the sky, piercing down into the earth and underworld, cracking open to let the dwarves emerge. are there dwarf civilizations in the sky too? do they come on purpose, or are they born on impact? their hair/beards are crystal, bodies stone; the carve runes on themselves. they seek to bring Law to the world and underworld.
    - are they creations of the Malakim? soldier-miners sent to attack the Underworld and bring law to the surface (also a pre-emptive defensive move against the coming Age of Night, creating bastions to stand until the dawn), now independent since the retreat of the ur-races.
    - legends of an advanced pre-falling star dwarven civilization in the heavens. did they choose to leave? were they sent? cast down? are there any still up there?

- some time after emerging, dwarves form an uneasy peace with the fey, largely eschewing the surface and conquering the depths

- dwarves use runes to create light, they are of the heavens, they don't see in the dark

- as they explored the underworld, they naturally found and explored the ruins of the elder races. they were the first to unlock and develop the magics of the elders, and a lot of the oldest magical writings and linguistics work comes from dwarves
- as a result of their unearthing, an archaeological and scholarly bent has been baked into dwarven culture since the beginning.
    - the encountered the dreaming elder dragons in their subterranean desolations. thus was born the enmity between dwarf and dragon, seekers after what dragons had stolen and hoarded from the elder days.
- they gravitated strongly to law magic, but made extensive use of chaos too in their creation-forges. entropy less so, but no potential art was ignored
- their magic is related to the three main elder branches: law, chaos, and entropy,
- absorbing some of the turkic/arabic flavors of the elders, dwarven language is semitic / middle eastern; play up the khazad-dum vibes

- in the twilight age, each dwarf hold was its own city-state and had its own culture, being far separated. They did maintain constant communication and had a sense of cultural cohesion via resonant crystal communicators made from shards of their geode-meteor-chariots

- upon death, they fossilize (return to stone); they grow more stony / crystalline in their old age

- dwarven "reality" is a bit more "solid" than everyon else's. They are more resilient to magic, particularly magic that alters the body, mind, or spirit. They are especially resilient to glamour.
