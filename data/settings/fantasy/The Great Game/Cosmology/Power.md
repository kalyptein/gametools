# Glamour

- based on the malleability of reality in the Age of Twilight.
- almost exclusively practiced by fey, who have the minds and nature to comprehend and manipulate it.
- within pockets of the Wode, artificers shape reality as they once did. Outside, one must produce an ephemeral piece of that reality to shape; some practitioners shape themselves, some shape focus items such as weapons, some emit an ephemeral "cloud" of glamour they can shape, or fragments of Wode that can be shaped into servitors.

Feats & Thresholds

gain power by achieving various feats:

- visit mystical places (on Earth, in dream / spirit, outer space, other planes, etc) and often do something ceremonial there to "lock it in".
- consume the parts of magical beasts/demons/angels/etc
- create and consume alchemy that perfects the body and soul
- perform ceremonial acts (ritual murder, symbolic rebirth, special marriage, sex, create works of art or craft, undergo ritual torture, etc)
- commune with cosmic beings/forces
- alter/imbue the body (piercings, maimings, scars, tattoos, perhaps with special materials/techniques)
- take on a secret name, assume a mystic identify and live within its mask/precepts, obey a taboo
- gain patronage of a mystic being, bind yourself to an occult brotherhood, bind yourself to a mystic place

ways to "go beyond" and seek power and secret knowledge

- ascend to the highest peaks (perhaps even hidden / metaphysical ones), perhaps groups of peaks with special associations. perhaps doing rituals as you go up or at the top, or undertaking the journey with certain taboos or limitations
- descending into the deepest sea or caves
- dying and coming back
- walking / recreating the legend / path of a hero or god
- transgressing taboos: commiting murder, etc
- enduring deadly peril / hardship and surviving
- eating the fruit / organs / nectar of mystical creatures or plants
- cultivating a quality in people and then stealing / consuming it (beauty, youth, love, etc), or even having them willingly sacrifice it to you
- marrying / having a child with / being born (perhaps symbolically reborn) of a mystical being
- sacrificing / abstaining from a normal part of being human (losing a sense, celibacy / castration, etc)

when you cross the threshold of a cosmic power / experiences, you might (some, all, or none)

- gain power
- pay a price / be broken or damaged

to endure the threshold of a power

- be strong enough in some capacity (a stat; enough intelligence, enough wisdom, will, discipline, toughness)
- make sacrifices to appease / balance it
- overcome a guardian or barrier to reach it
- endure an ongoing price or peril (hunted by a beast, living with a taboo or strange hunger, etc)
- be broken or damaged (kind of a spiritual crumple zone sacrificed to endure the power)
- make a pact, perform a service or periodic Teind or duty
- give up some part of your humanity (soul, memory, love, etc).
    - you can earn "enlightenment xp" by doing stuff (following your drives, visiting mystic places to prepare yourself, etc), these can be spent to offset the price, so you're earning and spending this to take the spiritual hit so you don't lose something more essential, or you've become strong enough to endure it), then you have to go earn more
- figure out a trick or puzzle to do it correctly (have been to the correct pilgrimage sites, know the chant, understand the nature of the threat, undergo a symbolic rebirth, have performed the right purification rituals, etc)
- have certain traits (be a virgin, a twin, have slain your own kin, seventh son of a seventh son, having died and returned, etc)

if may be possible to recover from a crushing threshold experience, regaining what you lost or removing some limit or price. see Gorogoa: contact with the god works but smites you, but a lifetime of seeking brings you back into harmony
