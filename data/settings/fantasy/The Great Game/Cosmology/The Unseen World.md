# The Great Gods

Playing the "game of divinity" that shapes the world. All are personified as 'she', but aren't individuals lesser beings interact with.

- Law: stasis / persistance, structure, hierarchy, relationship; depicted as an ornate, masked queen
- Chaos: creation, change, impermanence; depicted like a tattooed, primal shamness
- Entropy / Void / Dissolution: breakdown to nothingness or ground-state, void, change without replacement; depicted as a shrouded woman in black

Sages debate the ultimate nature of power and reality:

- Are the 3 Great Gods the sources of their respective power, with Kismet as a subordinate synthesis / creation?
- Is Kismet the source of all power, with the 3 Great Gods drawing on the resources she provides to play the game? Maybe Kismet even made its own players?
- Is there a ultimate source of reality, unknown to sages, above the Great Gods and their game?
- Is the whole personification of the Great Gods just a mortal (and ascended being) fallacy?

  

- Azimān (ah-zee-MAAN): the Great Gods ('Great Ones') collectively
    - Chahār-e Azimān (cha-HAAR eh ah-zee-MAAN): the Four Great Ones, a formal / theological phrasing.
    - Chahār (cha-HAAR): "the Four", a shorthand for the Great Gods
- Azim (ah-ZEEM): an individual Great God ('great', 'mighty')

  

Before the world had form, there were only the Chahār-e Azimān. Nizam drew the lines of order. Kismet cast the lots of destiny. Şekilsiz stirred the pieces into new shapes. And Dağılma waited, patient, to scatter them again.

The Afterlife

- The souls of the dead pass into the Ascended realm and into the keeping of a divinity.
    - the acquisition of souls grants their power to the one who holds them, much like ritual devotion but more permanent and thorough. Gods may woo with promises of paradise or endless revels, or seek to steal and compel, or make bargains and pacts.
    - The soul's affinities for the god and their domains governs this. The wrong affinity or weak devotion, or strong affinity for a different domain might send a soul to another destination (a worn-torn life and death might send you into the hands of the war-god whose armies slew you).
    - Souls dwell in the enigmatic paths of the Ascended realm, not so much "in a place".
    - Certain magics, pacts with demons, specific burial rites, etc can hijack or assist your desired journey. Malign beings might waylay a soul to devour or enslave it; burial with rites and talismans can help protect the soul. A soul can lose its way, or refuse the journey out of unfinished business, becoming a lost soul, perhaps returning as a ghost (particularly if it had some significant power).
        - the fallen in battle are often allowed to return to their people for, or even buried with rites by their killers, to avoid their return as vengeful ghosts (even if the true soul passes on, an aspect of its power and rage may return and become a vengeful spirit.
    - There are psychopomp ascended who can aid your journey, as well as mortal shamans, holy relics, sanctified burial places, etc. A vision-quest into the Ascended realm might liberate a soul from a wrongful claim.

- It takes time (days, weeks, etc) for a soul to finish departing its body and reach its destination. As it does, recalling it to speak, revive, etc becomes increasingly difficult. A truly departed spirit must be sought through vision-questing, or called forth with very powerful magic.

Dreams

- In dream, a soul "delocalizes" a bit and can touch the Ascended realm, a bit like it will do more fully in death.
- Thus dream is a place one might communicate with ascended beings, spirits of the dead, etc.

## Levels of Being

The cosmos has "levels of being", like the Qabalistic Four Worlds.

- Each layer has a limited ability to touch those layers above and below.
- The higher you go, the more wide-spread and profound, but more subtle, you influence over the world is, as well as the greater your insight / perception into the cosmos and the plays of the game / fate.

Layers

Highest / Summit (En Ustun)

- where the Great Gods exist and Kismet is played
- The Great Gods don't manifest directly, they shape the world through the game.

Sublime (Asil)

- Domain of the full-fledged gods
- The gods mostly work through agents (saints, servitors, demigods, heroes, anointed organizations, divine courts).
    - It takes a lot of preparation for a god to be able to directly work miracles in the world (often they create an avatar, effectively a demigod).
    - A god can throw caution to the wind and "lunge down" to affect the world, but it costs them a lot of ascended stature; an unwise manifestation by a weakened god might knock it down to demigod status until it can against make the long slow climb back up (if it manages; perhaps targeted by rivals and enemies).
- Entering the Sublime Realm should feel feel like vision-questing; symbolism and paths and barriers, more than concrete terrain, though iconic places can exist (palaces of gods, etc). The upper part of each divine court exists at this layer.

Ascended (Yukseldi)

- Domain of the saints, heroes, avatars, lesser aspects, and demigods, and the otherworlds of the Divine Courts
    - Ascended beings spend most of their time aloof from the material world, but can manifest fairly readily. They still prefer to work through heroes, organizations, and the divine courts or the like.
- The Ascended Realm is like a planar adventure, surreal high fantasy terrain suiting the local court's theme. Sanctums of ascended individuals, etc.
    - There are demiplanes and such, products of powerful individuals, or broken off courts (through upheaval or from dead / crumbling courts).

Material (Temel; 'base')

- the familiar world
- By accumulating kismet's power and gradually performing the workings needed (rituals, bindings, gaining worship (borrowing the scraps of power of worshippers, being lifted up by them) and building an anointed organization, a being can ascend to higher levels.
- The aid of a higher power can help "pull you up", easing the process, but not bypassing it. Thus saints may be elevated by their gods more readily than an independent being could become a demigod.
- Higher beings can also create emanations on lower tiers: avatars, etc, with less of their power and insight, but greater access to directly change the world.

  

- just as a god reaching down to affect the world is powerful but difficult to arrange, mortals vision-questing up are a kind of "inverse miracle", gathering power and bringing it back; a means of channeling the power of a higher layer with the localization and focus of a mortal agent.

Layers of Ascension

- mortal beings are bound to bodies, maximum localization, minimal affinity with facets of reality. Their souls can leave their bodies for a time (dreaming, vision-questing) or upon death. Death is not ascencion, as it confers no additional affinity with reality, just delocalization. Affinity and delocalization confer greater ability to perceive the patterns of the world, and thus influence them.
- souls out of body naturally exist in the ascended world, to manifest in the physical requires power, much as an ascended being would need. It is easier to contact the material world by communicating with (or possessing) a material being via their soul (in dream, etc). Weak manifestation is easier, essentially creating a vessel capable of channeling less power. Incautious manifestation can be dangerous; destruction of the vessel can leave the soul stranded in the material, cutting it away from the ascended world (for a spiritwalker, this severs the link back to their real body, for an ascended, it can cut away their power and leave them stranded in the mortal world again).
- ascended beings shed their bodies and move fully enter the ascended world, delocalized and possessing significant affinity with facets of reality.
- sublime being differ from the ascended because they have forged enough affinity and delocalized enough that they exist in multiple aspects, a distributed consciousness able to be more and perceive more of the world.
- the great gods are maximally delocalized, with affiniuty covering a third of reality and minds that pervade everything.
