




Tokens (Cyphers)

- there are "tokens", consumable objects like Numenera cyphers, one- or few-shot items with powers.

- e.g. a comb you throw that grows into a thicket, or a mirror into a pond, or a potion of flight, or whatever.

- all dreamers have a Kenning ability, letting them study tokens and learning their powers over brief or short or long rests.  complex apparatus might require a long-term project over downtime(s)

- often, magical beasts, once defeated can yield a token.  The dreamer can declare what property they are trying to capture and cut off part of the beast to incarnate that property.

- e.g. if you want a fire token and you cut out a dragon's heart, or you want poison so you cut off the scorpion's stinger or squeeze out some poison.  You make a Kenning roll to see if you capture what you meant to, or maybe it does something different within the same theme (or perhaps w/ a negative side-effect).

-  harvest plants with magical properties as tokens (Strange Horticulture was the inspiration; can use real Language of Flowers stuff, but with imaginary flowers).

- Maybe minerals?  Gems might have known properties, cribbed from real world lore.

- Some of these things might have to be harvested in the right way (under a full moon, with a silver sickle, etc).  Hand of Glory?  Other bits of people?  Perhaps a Kenning roll to see the potential within the dreamland and understand how to pluck it out.

- reagants / components could be made into tokens with sufficient craft

- tokens might be traded in markets in the bigger settlements.

  

- alternate take, instead of consumables, they grant a constant benefit (passive trait, activated ability, bonus, etc) for a certain duration.  You can't potion-hoard, because it's like you just consume them on the spot when you get them.  if you don't use the ability or don't want it right out the gate, you can turn it into a small generic bonus (some healing, etc).  The token may be something you literally consume (dragon's blood, etc) or it can be an item that just has a power for a certain span of time, but you could lose or trade it, it's just got an expiration date.

Artifacts
* powerful, unique or small set
* have their of purpose / destiny / desire; they collaborate with bearers for a time
* their power must be awoken (intentionally or by accident) by a bearer, and tiers can be unlocked, growing its power
