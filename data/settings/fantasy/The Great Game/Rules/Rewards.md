
XP

- There are tiers of XP (e.g. mundane, heroic, paragon, and epic)
- You advance within your tier by getting that kind of xp. The next kind lower is still useful, it lets you broaden and refine your talents, but won't propel you to up the tier.
    - e.g. a heroic character needs heroic xp to work their way up the tier, but mundane xp can still help fill in skills, gain minor abilities, etc.
- you have to reach the top of your tier, then earn some of the next higher tier's xp.
    - maybe being at the top of your tier means you just need a little higher-tier xp; if you get enough higher-tier xp, you can immediately elevate without being top-of-tier
- it's possible to have much higher tier xp that you can't really use. A mundane person somehow imbued with epic power doesn't immediately being an epic hero. They might sometimes manifest wild surges of power or have flashes, but they have to grow into it before they can really use it. But if they worked their way up through paragon, they might suddenly "come into their own" when they get powerful enough, as they finally tap into that epic power properly.
