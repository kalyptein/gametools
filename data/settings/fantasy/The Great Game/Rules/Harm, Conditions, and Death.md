Death

- when someone dies, they undergo a harrowing.  fail it, and they're "dead" for good.  pass and they return with a scar.  their death is also foisted onto the dreamlands itself, darkening or twisting it, destroying / threatening / worsening something they care about, or just the general circumstance, or perhaps some threat or cost comes to them (they might also just lose an item, rank, or something else they cared about).
- even if you survive a harrowing, you gain some Shadow Debt?


- some forms of death may be off the table, but accrue Shadow Debt or some other type of Harrowing currency.  Dreamers never die of hunger or thirst for lack of food, though they will become terribly weak and suffer greatly, to the point the trauma becomes Shadow Debt.  Thus there is a price for the experience / failure, but it doesn't lead to a Harrowing.
	- maybe there is a quantity of Shadow Debt / Harrowing currency so great that it prompts an immediate Harrowing type roll.  You can't truly die, but you might immediately suffer mutation / scarring, or push some of the trauma off onto the Dreamlands itself.

expanded ideas for death
* when you die, you undergo a harrowing type experience
	* depending on player preference, there might be risk of perma-death, or maybe you always do (but NPC dreamers might not always).
* ideally you return from death with a boon (maybe also changes, or mutations).  if you die too often, you don't get this and it just sucks
	* when you die, you gain 1 death point and 1 boon if your death point total is less than your Level+Paragon.  If your Level+Paragon is 3 or more above your death point total, mark 2 death points and get 2 boons.  If gaining a death point would take your total above Level+Paragon, you don't mark it and don't get a boon; it just sucks.
	* if there is a chance of perma-death, it's based on a roll that benefits the higher your Level+Paragon is greater than your death points.  A big enough buffer might guarantee successful return.
* boons from death represent glimpses into the depths of the subconscious, cosmic wisdom, power wrested from your Shadow, renewed determination and will, a brush with an Arcana, etc.
	* stat points, new powers, mutations, magic items, etc
