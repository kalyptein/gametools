Syzygy (gotta be something cool to do with this term/idea)
* Astonomy: A syzygy is the nearly straight-line alignment of three or more celestial bodies in a gravitational system, such as the Sun, Earth, and Moon. This alignment is the driving force behind eclipses (both solar and lunar) and significantly impacts the Earth's tides.
* Psychology: Carl Jung used syzygy to describe a union of opposites or a complementary pairing of archetypes.
* Philosophy & Theology: It is used in Gnosticism and the philosophy of Vladimir Solovyov to denote a "close union" of contrasting or divine elements.


## The Arcana
### The Fool

- Motifs
	- the dreamland will be in its "default" state
	- the season will be a mild late-spring / summer / early fall
- Gifts
	- welcomes new dreamers
	- gifts simple, sturdy gear, hearty food, and cryptic adventure hooks.
	- perhaps you can't really die on this first journey?
- Shadow
	- childish petulance, malice, and destructiveness

### The Magician / Magus

- Motifs
	- wondrous and overt magical phenomena in the dreamland (more than usual).
	- a time to test or break the limits of magic and achievement, do the impossible, seek enlightenment or transcendence.
	- magic is common, people are eldritch beings (genasi, beastfolk, etc) (don't want normal dreamlands to be boring, so maybe this stuff is normal and the magus is reflected in some other way?)
	- seasons pass but are heightened, weird and wondrous versions of themselves, or totally unknown and new un-seasons
- Gifts
	- the arcana grants each dreaming a magical boon (item, ability, etc) that lasts through the cycle.
- Shadow
	- hubris, tampering with things man was not meant to know, ushering in great and terrible works (nukes, etc); just because you can doesn't mean you should

### The High Priestess

- Motifs
	- dreamlands are full of vistas, spectacle; art flourishes, artists, bards, etc are held in high esteem, societies are gripped by aesthetics.
	- seeking wonders, drama, or glory and immortalizing it in art is rewarded.
	- dreamer creation of art can serve as a divination or prophecy
	- seasons pass normally, each a vivid and distinct aesthetic experience
- Gifts
	- arcana offers prophecy for info and questing to make it come true (for xp, reward, achieving an goal)
- Shadow
	- tearing yourself and other things down with endless criticism and mockery

### The Empress

- Motifs
	- the dreamland will be orderly, fertile and bountiful, fields, game in forests.
	- a green spring or bountiful summer or harvest autumn.
	- authority figures will tend to be female (queens, priestesses, witches, matriarchs), tradition reigns over explicit laws, gifts and folkways over commerce.
	- things are old and established, worn into the land and society
- Gifts
	- the empress offers largess to suplicants, greater according to the social status of the dreamer (titles earned, conquests made, etc).  also grants to parents and heads of household.
- Shadow
	- the devouring mother?  the mother resentful of her own children, cannibalizing their futures to fill the hole in herself?

### The Emperor

- Motifs
	- the dreamland is full of hierarchy, kings, lords, high priests; there are political and military struggles between them.
	- much law and regulation, raising of monuments and great edifices.
	- authority figures will tend to be male (archpriests, magi, kings, generals, patriarchs)
	- seasons pass, but not obstructing war and diplomacy.
- Gifts
	- the emperor offers largess to suplicants, greater according to the social status of the dreamer (titles earned, conquests made, etc).
- Shadow
	- ruining things through over-controlling-ness?  the tighter you squeeze, the more star systems slip through your fingers

### Justice

- Motifs
	- those who seek vengeance or justice will be aided; could be the dreamers and/or someone who seeks it against them.
	- in settlements, there will be important trials or itinerant judges will be present, forces of law or retribution will be abroad.
	- it will be a mild summer, with orderly and hospitable weather, with periodic terrible storms to dramatically backdrop vengeance or peril.
	- bonus xp for resolving grudges or gaining justice.
- Gifts
	- Justice will hear questions of how to seek justice, vengeance, or reconciliation and offer advice.
- Shadow
	- self-destructive, self-corrosive pursuit of vengeance, getting even, getting your due

### The Hermit

- Motifs
	- divinations are enhanced this cycle.
	- the weather is clear and calm, in any season.
	- there will be seers and diviners, conclaves of academics, libraries, archives

- Gifts
	- hermit will offer answers and advice on seeking knowledge, might offer quests to retrieve knowledge for him.
- Shadow

### The Wheel of Fortune / Fortune

* Motifs
	* it will be a random season in the dreamlands, mild or wild, and the seasons turn unpredictably
	* the lands are full of games, contests, wagers
	* rulers, merchants, generals make risky plays; matters of great import are settled by chance and wager
- Gifts
	- A great opportunity will be presented to you or great misfortune will assail you, or both (not sure which until it happens

- Shadow
	- risking all on foolish chances, gambling what you can't afford to lose; failure to prepare for the future (grasshopper-ness), foolish trust in fortune/fate, wasting resources

### The Hanged Man / The Sacrifice

- Motifs
	- the dreamlands are prevalent with prisons, vaults, oaths, contracts, chains, possession, exiles, durances.
	- sacrifices and wiergilt are demanded, trials faced.
	- it is a clear summer, a brambly autumn, or an ice-cased winter.
	- the world is full of gates, barriers, bandits, toll-collectors, brambles, wood walls, and other obstacles to going off roads or crossing boundaries.
	- entering and leaving settlements is strangely hard.
	- politics and tradition are stagnant, or poised on the edge of sudden breaks and revolutions
	- bindings are easier (oaths, imprisonment, contracts), escape/breaching bonds is harder.
- Gifts
	- the hanged man will take something important from each dreamer (a possession, a power, a quality like beauty, voice, sight, etc; or burdening with something they must carry, a disability, a curse, a thing shackled to them), returning it at the end of the cycle, perhaps even offering a reward for enduring without.
- Shadow
	- apathy, fear of failure and thus never beginning or not advancing, self-sabotage

### Death

- Motifs
	- endings stalk the land, but not large scale ruin (the tower).
		- people are old, or nearing the end of periods of their life (graduation, moving, etc).
		- there are wars, transfers of power, illness. 
		- or there is the aftermath of death and change, heirs taking over, confusion in the absence, power vacuums.
	- morbid / gothic motifs
	- it is the change between seasons, swinging between characteristic weather of the new and the old, marks of the old within the new or the new emerging from the old.
	- Gifts
		- the arcana offers advice and aid to those willing to shed some part of themselves and move toward something new.
		- all dreamers are marked by death.  a skeletal hand or Hel-scarred half-face, clad in a shroud, pallid, gaunt, aged, white-haired, etc.
- Shadow
	- moth-to-the-flame fascination leading to self-annihilation
