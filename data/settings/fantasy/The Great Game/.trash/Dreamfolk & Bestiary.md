Wizards (Sages?) & Witches

- there are magi; powerful wizards, they are associated with towers.  they build them and ever-expand them (either up or down), often impossibly.  weird chambers, experiments, prisons and vaults.  they can be approached for sage advice or magical aid, but usually demand payment in service or unusual items, or perhaps lore or secrets.  they are somewhat place-bound to their towers; they may reach out through sendings, conjurered servants, custos, or projections, but much of their power is linked to their tower.  sometimes they die or transcend, leaving an empty tower behind.  towers may have a domain around them, ruled or simply shaped as a side-effect.  there may be minor magi, hedge-magi, who are less of a big deal but more approachable and less self-absorbed.  appolonian vibe, writing, magic circles, ornate rituals, complex instruments.  usually have a particular motif, like artifice, alchemy, astrologer, etc.  some serve kings, cities, etc, some are aloof, some malign.  if the tower is destroyed, the wizard perishes or loses all their power, perhaps carried off by beings they owe debts to.

- there are witches: they rule natural domains (bays in the sea, mountain peak, old forest, graveyard, caves, barrow, etc.  they often make deals, often taking payment in things from the seeker (blood, voice, firstborn, eye, happiness, etc) or things retreived for them from elsewhere.  dyonisian vibe, blood, primal night, caves, fire, bone and antler, hunger.  they have weaknesses, banes, taboos.

* these beings are expressions of arcana?  the wizards are of the Magus?  witches of the Moon, the Devil, etc?  are there others?

witches and magi are both subtypes of Dominus / Domina / Dominum figures, rulers of domains who draw power from them, are limited to them, and express their nature.  could also take the form of Apex Monsters (Forest Spirit, Minotaur, etc) or other things
- towers for magi link in to old ideas about things like the Tower of Babel, symbols of mastery and also hubris
- there could be Old Law-style rules in these domains, things the dominum imposes or desires?

The Dead
- the dead: dreamers and otherwise, can be sought in the dreamland's Underworld and called up from the Unconscious to parley with.

- dybbuk-type lost souls, perhaps in self-imposed states of suspension or self-affliction.  could be karmic / self-flagelation

- the dead are in closer touch with the Unconscious, unmoored in time and space to a degree, able to divine and perceive things about the world and the dreamlands that living humans can't.

- dead dreamers incarnated in the dreamlands as iconic but static beings.
	- Are they always dead (like a ghost, lich, etc) or can they be living?

- there are amalgam ancestral dead of various cultures and even pre-homo sapiens humanity

- hosts of semi-real Unborn, a swarm of proto-souls.  They are even more in touch with the Unconscious, and even the Arcana, than normal dead, and can be unparalelled oracles.  Can also be unpredictable, strange, powerful (because of their proximity to the Arcana), and perilous.  Some might incarnate directly into many-headed or swarm monsters, going direct to iconic status without having lived.  They might be curious about living experience, depanding memories from those they encounter, or trying to possess them.

saurians
* a sapient species (or more than one?) in the Jurassic period, extinct now.  The only remnants are dreamers who have remained within the dreamlands after death (so pretty powerful former-dreamers).
* There might be tombs of these ancient saurians, or ruins of their dream-cities, or other works.  There may be some living (or undead) remnants.

apshai
* sapient eusocial insects from the high-oxygen era (bees?).  in dream, they appear as singular beings with swarm of bodies, because they were sapient at the hive level.
* like saurians, they are otherwise gone, as the atmosphere can no-longer support them; or these are atavisms dreaming up racial memory from present-day hives.

cephalopods, kraken
* sapient deep-water squid/octopi.  Very old, but still exist down below.  Rare, solitary, weird.
* They have no society, no tech, but are aware and dreaming, and in the dreamland can be quite powerful, acquiring magic from studying the ruins of their predecessors on the ocean floor, vast fields of obelisks, crop-circle spirals of stones and coral laid out, etc.
* krakens are soul-sign / eidolon of the cephalopod species
* octopi have nine brains (one in each arm, one central)
	* in the dreamlands, they are a nine-part entity.  there is the main cephalopod entity, the central brain, and eight emanations that are not quite full, independent beings on their own.  they bond with other dream creatures (human, animal, demon, etc), taking the form of that creature, perhaps magically mutated, with a single symbiotic tentacle.  they ally with / serve the central entity
