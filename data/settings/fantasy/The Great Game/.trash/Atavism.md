Atavism
- atavism: you tap into the instincts and pre-human parts of your mind.
	- manifests differently for different classes.
	- the risk is always that you might have trouble exercising full human reason while tapping into it, or get carried away by instinctual impulses.
	- because this is the mental world, tapping in can result in physical transformation
* call up a beast companion / familiar / mount
	* all are familiar-like, in that they are an expression of you, a spirit you can recall if they die
	* shamans can have familiars, that are companions drawn from one of their Names?
* shapeshifting
* taking on beast traits, riding a beast's mind
* communing w/ nature ("divination" based on a beast's attunement to the world)
* speaking with beasts, calling and commanding beasts
