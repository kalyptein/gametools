Drives
- Protection
	- To protect your charges (family, companions, clan, nation; from peril, from want, from itself).
- Gain
	- To reap the spoils of conflict (riches, land, power, etc), as raider, conqueror, or mercenary.
- Glory
	- To be lauded and remembered for your deeds.
- Thrill
	- To feel the thrill of danger and challenge.
- Domination
	- To dominate and control.  To be free of others' control.
- Duty
	- To faithfully carry out your duty.  To be the strong right arm of your liege / people / faction / ideology.
- Survival
	- To survive in the face of life's hardships and perils.
- Vengeance / Justice
	- To smite the wicked (by whatever standard), or to gain vengeance.
- Slaughter
	- To deal death, destruction, and/or pain.
- Excellence
	- To achieve perfection in martial skill.
- Death Wish
	- To seek self-destruction in battle and danger.
