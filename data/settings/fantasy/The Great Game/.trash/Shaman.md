- decide what you want to achieve, DM sets DIF
- pile up modifiers, roll (tokens, strong / weak presence of a Name, shaman's casting bonus or Name-investment, panoply, asking the Name to do something against its nature / desires, etc)
- if you beat the DIF, you do it for modest price (spend an effort, etc?), enough excess and it's free
- fall short and you must make up the difference by taking on fallout
	- personal harm or affliction
	- the powers you called range out of your control (collateral damage or complication)
	- consume / weaken / extinguish / steal the presence of the power you called (sun goes dark, rain here means drought elsewhere, to make rain here steals water from trees and creatures withering them)
	- other powers are roused to ire by your working and react
	- you have to burn some caster currency (more effort / mana, burn a token or magic item, future casting penalty until rested)
	- you can't speak to this Name again until you rest / reestablish the connection / make ammends (or less than can't at all, maybe just a penalty to this Name)
	- the effect is less efficaious than you wanted (partial / limited) effect (some offset), or completely ineffectual  (more offset)
	
	- you can choose to accept harm/afflicition, lost touch with the Name, and burn currency to offset.  If you still have more offset to pay, the rest is random or up to the DM>
		- before casting, you can "give the power leave to run wild", gaining a bonus to the roll but guaranteeing collateral damage.  If fallout happens and other powers react, that will be worse than normal instead, as will burnout of the power.  less/non- effective won't happen

cyphers have an aspect (a Name) and a power (minor, moderate, major, magnificent).

cyphers can be consumed for a bonus on a casting roll based on their power.

Moderate cyphers can be consumed to speak to a Name you don't innately possess.  Major cyphers can do both that and provide another type of bonus as for a Moderate cypher (e.g. casting bonus).  Magnificent cyphers can be used to speak to their Name without being consumed.

can cyphers stand in for Effort?  Are we using that?

cyphers can be consumed as part of Rituals or Crafting.

cyphers can be consumed to provide a bit of their Name when it would otherwise be absent (calling up fire from a drop of dragon blood when there is no fire present).

using a cypher for a weaker benefit than it could provide (a Major cypher to provide a Moderate benefit, lets you also get other benefits, treat as 1 weaker, and consume, or "keep the chance" as a weakened cypher.  if the cypher is 2 steps stronger than the benefit, it can provide it without being depleted (if you start calling on multiple benefits, that raises the effective demand and can deplete it).

do you have Names or magical feats (healing, divination, can't affect children, etc) that are taboo, that you can never call, and whose presence might oppose you?  reflective of your personality or the limits you place on yourself?

being in a place rich with a Domain makes that magic easier (Ruin in a Storm, Fire in a Forge); in places that it is weak or lacking, that magic is harder (Water in the Desert, Heavens in a Cave)


other things around you might respond when you speak as well, if they have an "opinion".  in a place strong in darkness, the dark might respond when you try to call up light

fall short and you must either expend extra Effort, suffer penalties on future rolls, lose access to that Name for a time, suffer personal backlash, cause fallout / side-effects / collateral damage, the effect is weaker than desired, or the spell fails entirely (you still spend the Effort, but maybe for less time, or you lose the Name but for less time?)

If you roll enough over, you can spend the excess for benefits.  make the Effort commit-time shorter or remove it altogether (so cantrips would become cheaper / free), amplify the effect, duration, etc.
