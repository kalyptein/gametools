### Nature
##### Magnitude

* least (1-4 hp, minor effects)
* lesser (1 - 4 HD, 1-2 level spells)
* greater (5 - 8 HD, 3-4 level spells)
* grand (9 - 12 HD, 5-6 level spells)
* sublime (13+ HD, rare and special "artifact-level" spells)
	* never found randomly, these are "artifact-level" spirits

##### Nature

* corporeal
	* subtype
		* organic / biological
		* elemental / construct
* intangible
	* generally lack the ability to manipulate the physical world; somewhat safe from material harm, but often vulnerable to things like magic, other spirits, magic weapons, fire / energies, etc and of course their banes.
	* they can usually harm other spirits
* inhabitor? symbiote? (parasite / possessor; needs a vessel)
	* subtype
		* autonomous parasite (material creature, can exist (at least for atime) outside a host, but can't do much)
		* infection (drug, drink, egg, larva, poison) (inert outside a host)
		* spirit (a weak spirit form, can exist (at least for a time) outside a host)
	* inhabitors always have an effect on their host
		* side-effect, price, impose some facet of themselves (intelligence, speech, personality, motives...also almost certainly banes, tabboos, etc; and there's always a tell it's in the host, sometimes subtle, sometimes obvious)
	* inhabitors often bestow their powers on their host (might be under host's control, or the inhabitor may control them as it sees fit, perhaps taking orders/suggestions)

Extras
* affects base "cost", choose 1
	* no change (purely a bonus)
	* requires a limit to offset
	* reduces power (potentially down a full tier, but often less)
* standard extras
	* Affects Others (not just you)
	* Affects X (works on something normally immune)
	* Broadcast (propagates through an unexpected medium, such as writing, music, etc that encodes it)
	* Burst (AoE)
	* Contagious
	* Defensive (can be used to block / counter attacks)
	* Effect (duplicates a related power)
	* Level Duration (gives it "level" duration, instead of the usual)
	* Passengers (movement power can carry others)
	* Ranged (extends close range to extended)
	* Rangeless (extends power to rangeless)
	* Reversible
	* Secondary Effect (is a carrier for a second power; a strike with a status effect, etc)
	* Slow Recovery (10x time or special condition to remove effect)

Limits
* affects base "cost", choose 1
	* no change (purely a defect)
	* pays for an extra
	* increase power (potentially up to a full tier, but often less)
* limits can be minor or major
	* e.g. affects metal (minor) or affects precious metals (major)
	* e.g. affects animals (minor) or affects bats (major)
* standard limits
	* animals / subgroup only
	* blocked by X (something common / easy wards against this power)
	* burnout (limited use)
	* close range (touch / very close)
	* concentration
	* constant
	* degrades (weakens with each use)
	* exclusive (can't use other powers at the same time)
	* extra only (choose an extra, you get that power, not the base power)
	* line of sight
	* max only (no fine control / power modulation)
	* only X (only works in a special circumstance (at night, while angry, etc), against a particular target (men, wood, etc))
	* others only (can't use this on yourself)
	* performance (there are complex / lengthy actions needed to use it)
	* preparation (you need to set thing up ahead of time, get reagents, etc)
	* source (you can only affect what's present, can't generate the energy / matter / etc)
	* temporary (short-lived)
	* tiring (harms / weakens you when you use it)
	* uncontrolled (sometimes / only goes off unbidden)
	* unpredictable (2-in-6 chance the power doesn't activate when called on)
	* unstable (if used under stress, 2-in-6 chance if activates randomly)
