- fuse words together into spells during downtime research
	- Minor = 2 words, Major = 3 words, Mighty = 4? 5? words
	- with some difficulty you might be able to break a spell back apart into words, but in a lossy way (only get 1 word back?)
	- spells are magical "objects", you can trade them, but can't duplicate (could use one as a model for recreating, given the component Words)
	- you might need Power insights to make higher tier spells, or they must be forged in the presence of a font / conjunction?
- must have at least a verb and a noun
- some words are definitely verbs (destroy, perceive, etc), some are nouns
	- if the noun has a characteristic action, if can be used as a verb for that, as long as the resulting spell doesn't clash with the underlying noun
		- fish swim and breath water, the sun shines and rises and sets, fire burns
	- nouns can also be used as an adjective (fire = hot)
	- verbs can be used as gerunds? (fire = burning)
	- are there conjunctions (and, but, or?) or can they be created / inferred? (maybe it's a bit random?)
- when you fuse the words, the GM rolls some quirks (duration, controllability, range, side-effects, price, casting time, tags, etc)
	- roll more than are needed, and the researched can try to curate the most desirable ones
- spell ends up with a tier, a casting difficulty, degree of effect

- can "carry" a max of 21 spells at a time (or some total number of words worth of spells?)  (21, the number of major arcana - 1 ?)
	- shadowdark style casting
	- exhaust 7 (one third) and lose casting until a long rest
	- this is just a sanity check on option paralysis, not meant as a big limitation
	- you can still have more bound up, able to be swapped in or cast as rituals

* GM creates spells by rolling on Knave's lists
	* Wizard names give the spell a characteristic twist reflective of the wizard's personality / magical style.  there may be rules about what kinds of spells they can occur within
		* could be names of witches, demons, demigods, heroes, great rulers, legendary figures, archetypal figures, celestial bodies, etc as well as wizards
		* a spell can be from a wizard without having their name in it
	* if uncertain what a spell might do, roll a Form from my magic analysis list as guidance (or an ars magica technique/form, or a quality/effect from knave, or a Sundered Isles core oracle of Action/Theme/Descriptor/Focus)
* GM determines mechanics
* power tiers: lesser, greater, mighty, or Tier 1-5?
	* access lesser at 1st, greater at 5th, mighty at 9th
* roll some tags (controllability, price, casting time, foci, duration, etc)
* try to have the spells function in a very "natural language" way (kind of like Dolemwood faerie magic was working out).
	* Durations, ranges, sizes, etc relate to the logic of the spell, or to easily measurable things (what you can pick up, what you can see, what your can hide in your closed hand, as long as you remain inside the area, until you sleep, etc).

* spells are "objects", you can give them away but not copy them
	* they dwell, bound into the pool of your mind, like fish, living spirits dredged from the sea of the unconscious.  they will return there when you die.
* a shaman can break down a spell and harvest a single word from its name (which may instead be it's Tier, 1 or 2 Power words)
	* create new spells by supplying all the words and accessing a font or conjunction of sufficient power and aspect, and paying any other costs
* can go fishing for existing spells, paying with insights (not words) gained from exploration, research, experience, and discovery
	* vision-questing?
* 
* you can hold 21 spells netted in your mind at a time; readily accessible.  the rest can be accessed by ritual casting, or swapped in for something else
	* roll to cast like shadowdark, merciful magic guarantees 1 cast before a spell can become exhausted on failed cast
	* if 7 become exhausted, you can't cast more spells.
