Shadow-Calling
- you call on your Shadow, the self-destructive part of yourself, for power / insight / aid.
	- the shadow likes not only self-destruction, but it's also the part of humanity that likes things break and crumble, that feels sublime awe at mass destruction, that likes fire, that likes to witness pain and failure generally.
- The price will come due.
- the form of aid varies with your class.
	* often amplifies malice and destruction


general shadow-magic
- you can invest in shadow magic and add some of its malice to your own efforts to damage and destroy
- need at least a little Shadow Debt, and the more you have the more powerful your ability is?
	- using shadow magic (for inevitably destructive ends) helps stave off afflictions from your own shadow debt, like making interest payments
- Sometimes using an ability gives or risks giving Shadow Debt.
- trickster: poisons, backstabs, mockery, defaming lies
- warrior: strikes, shaming a victim, intimidation
- shaman: destruction, ruin, undoing, unmaking
