# The "Mandate of Heaven"

- An extreme instance of kismet's power accumulating in a place / organization. A race, nation, kingdom, etc may gain something like the Mandate of Heaven, imposing its paradigm on a portion of the world.
- In time, it can slip, passing to another if not carefully maintained, and the old hegemon fades. Sometimes the entire underlying nature of the world can change.

- - The timeless, dream-like world of the Wode of the elves fell, replaced by the human world.
    - The Seven Cities of Hell under ascendant diabolic rulers (god/demigod being, once human who pursued power and evolved into devils) seeks to impose a new order upon the lands.
    - The Underworld has no overarching order; individual domains can be shaped, but it is too much a realm of chaos and entropy.
