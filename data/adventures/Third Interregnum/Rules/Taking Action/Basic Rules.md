Checks
* 2d6+skill vs DIF
	* base DIF: 8
* effect = margin
	* effect 0: weak, effect 1-2: normal, effect 2-3: strong, effect 4+: crit (some rolls have specific meaning for effect, this is default)
* modifiers
	* slow & steady: +1, increase 1 step on timespan
	* quick & dirty: -1, decrease 1 step on timespan
	* non-proficient but possible: -2
		* includes off-label / questionable uses of a skill
		* 1 stamina (stress) to attempt?
* details
	* Pushing
		* costs 1 stamina (stress), escalates the attempt (potentially with unavoidable consequences), reroll check, failure = really bad outcome
		* generally not compatible with slow & steady (lose that bonus if you had it on the previous roll)
	* piggybacking
		* use sum of Effect for outcome
	* assistance
