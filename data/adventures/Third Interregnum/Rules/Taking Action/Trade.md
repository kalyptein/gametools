Markets
* Each system usually has a single effective market.
	* If there are any remote locations within the system, far from the rest of civilization, they might have additional market modifiers on top of the general system values.
	* This could also hold for a balkanized system.  Certain conditions might apply to some nation(s) and not others (some might be warring, one might control the local source of an important resource that the others are hungry for, etc)
* Goods Table
	* 10 goods that are often available here
* Friction
* Trouble
	* rating
	* table of mishaps
* Events
	* patrons / contacts, rumors, political / cultural, natural, economic, criminal, legal
* Market Modifiers
	* basic: shaped by production and demand
	* transient: caused by fads, wars, bubbles, shortages, gluts, laws, diplomatic shifts, tarriffs, cartels/monopolies, regulations, etc
	* some goods may be illegal or restricted (candidates for smuggling, if still desired)
* Local Currency
	* small outposts and non-nation entities usually accept the currency of one or more nearby major polities
	* even when multiple systems share a currency, it can take time for all the handshaking to shift large quantities
	* small sums of currency can be held in local banks / institutions without too much trouble, assuming some level of rule of law.  large ones are increasingly likely to be plundered one way or another while you're away, unless you have local holdings to tend them
* Status w/ various factions and individuals
	* can ask / be asked for favors, they may target you with trouble, etc
	* maybe use something like a -3 to +3 status like BitD, with a few points of favor per status
	* calling a favor costs a little favor, doing one raises it
* Holdings
	* notable local employees / partners
* Lifestyle Costs
	* tiers for individuals
	* also "lifestyle" for ships (docking, etc), only gets paid if you are parked more than a month, otherwise it's rolled into Friction
	* with the right Holdings, you could negate the need for ship and/or personal lifestyle costs
* Market Limits
	* buy: on average, 1 ton of a given good per 5000 inhabitants of the system
	* sell: unlimited, within reason

What's on the Market
* roll 2 per month that you are able to find decent deals on
	* can roll 2 more, but +1 Friction for all purchase deals this month as you pass on the best deals to hunt for other, less optimal ones.  You can still buy the original goods type, but with the elevated Friction.  increased Friction doesn't affect sales.  Max Friction increase is +4; at that point you can get any goods on the system's Goods Table.
* some big markets might have 3 or 4 goods available at a time (or 1d2+1); a dinky one might only have 1 (or 1d2)

Making a Deal
* lining up a deal takes a week, multiple deals and market research can be folded together, headed by different characters
* Expertise
	* assemble from across a trusted group of characters
	* = INT | CHA + Connections + (Persuasion/2, round down) + cultural proficiency - 2
		* or just fold the -2 into the trade table by shifting the values up 2 (so the middle is 12-13, not 10-11)
	* cultural proficiency: a rating much like a skill, running from unfamiliar (rank 0) to native (rank 3), with familiar (rank 2) being about as high as an off-worlder generally gets.
		* like all proficiencies, this can be raised through downtime and adventuring experience
* roll to determine the adjusted cost
	* buying: 3d6 - Expertise + Friction
	* selling: 3d6 + Expertise - Friction
	* decide if you want to go through with the deal or not
* trouble
	* ??? trouble happens regardless of whether you go through with the deal or not
		* some troubles would be null if there's no deal (shoddy cargo, etc), but others could apply anyway (criminals muscling in, legal trouble, etc)
	* new trouble ideas
		* buyer's/seller's remorse
		* etc
* a deal also has a chance of a "quirk", kind of like trouble, but not negative
	* make a contact, hear a rumor, get some kind of bonus thrown in (intentionally or not), other party is interested in future dealings, other party wants to pay with goods rather than credits, a hot tip (+A to market research or roll a new good w/o increasing Friction, deal has faction status implications, etc)
