
### The Guild of Hunters

* began during the First Imperium as a branch of the theo-state rooting out heretics.  Survived the fall of the empire as an independent force.
* granted recognition by the Second Imperium, where it hunted criminals and rebels against the state, but was also used by nobles for assassinating each other.  survived the fall again.
* slotted nearly into the Third Imperium as bounty-hunters for hire, taking on their modern form and monetary focus.  assassination faded into the background as a deniable service.
* 
* now independent again, peddling their services to system governments, syndicates, corps, etc, weaving in and out of legal activity
* vast, obscure legacy, baroque and secretive traditions, stockpiles of resources (often forgotten, even by the guild)
* apprentice-journeyman-master system.
	* a master trains an apprentice, who aids him.  the apprentice graduates to being a journeyman, roam and doing his own work, but often doing a stint with other masters (a renowned master may have a whole posse of journeymen at any given time), aiding them in return for experience and networking.  eventually they pull off a big enough bounty or kill or service to the guild and are acclaimed by enough masters to become a master themselves.

## Trade Monks, Ordo Mediatorum

* a riff on the Exchange from SWN
* monks conditioned to serve as perfect neutral middlemen, unfettered by greed for themselves (ego is displaced onto the survival and maintenance of their order), or morality relating to what they're intermediating (hostages, crime, etc), with discretion and neutrality.
	* the order takes a 5% tithe or similar fee of exchanges they assist
	* created by the Third Imperium to serve as couriers, mediators, go-betweens, escrow holders, notaries and witnesses, etc for the trade houses.  
	* they have an order-militant branch that serves as guards and assassins, protecting the order
	* vast knowledge of secret dealings, but they never divulge; although they do use it to keep the order secure.
* still functioning in the new interregnum, though they aren't everywhere
	* they provide some banking services, letters of credit, safe deposit for interstellar travelers
	* offer simple and not cheap accommodations for travelers, but very discrete
* plenty of monasteries were sacked during the collapse, and more might be lost with their treasures still intact (especially hidden depositories not in obvious view).  likely guarded with all manner of traps and security.
	* the monks would pay well for recovery, and may be enemies of looters (or maybe they wouldn't care enough?)
* without a "legitimate" master in the form of the empire, they have channeled their latent self-interest into protecting and advancing the order itself.
* 
* the monks cultivate a mix of telepathy and precognition and cyber-hypercognition to predict markets, politics, and read the trustworthiness and schemes of others

* there is a splinter sect called the Brothers of the Empty Hand
	* might have been produced by disruption of the conditioning and psi/hypercog-training of the monks, leaving them outside the control / "us-ness" of the monk subject
	* they kind of worship the Invisible Hand, broadening it into the idea of Kismet.  They are obsessed with reading and understanding it and its patterns.
	* some communities sustain small hermitages of religio-autistics, benefiting from their pronouncements.  others are kept and exploited by powerful patrons
