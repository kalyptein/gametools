## Level

* 0th - 10th
	* Above 10th, gain paragon ranks, with different benefits.
* Level MOD
	* LVL = level / 2, rounding up (+1 to +5)

## Stats
---
* For adventurers, stats range from 3 to 18 (may go higher for monsters).
* Stat MOD = (stat value - 10) / 2, rounding down (-4 to +4)
	* STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
* Stats can take damage from various effects (poison, magic, wounds, etc).
	* This can cause a stat's MOD to change, as well as other things calculated from it.
* Note
	* Stat bonuses, even when composed of multiple stats are capped at +4.
		* e.g. attack, casting
	* The total bonus, once additions from class, talents, magic items, etc are applied, can exceed +4.

Strength
* Feats of strength, athletics, rough climbing, swimming
* Wield forceful weapons, grapple.
* Stand fast against pushes, throws, etc, pull free from grapples and entanglement
* Carry gear

Dexterity
* Stealth, sleigh of hand, tinkering, sheer climbing, acrobatics, contortionism
* Wield finesse weapons.
* Improve your defense when not in Heavy armor.
* Dive for cover, keep your footing and balance, react quickly to danger, evade beams and blasts, slip out of bonds and grapples

Constitution
* Endure poison, disease, bodily curses, privation, and fatigue
* Take hits and recover from harm
* Carry gear

Intelligence
* Recall and apply lore and the cunning arts, feats of memory and cogitation, decipher clues and mysteries, study and learn
* Counter malign spells and magical effects when you have a protective talisman or implement at the ready
* Cast arcane magic

Wisdom
* Spot details and hidden things (and people), wildcraft, read a person, assess a situation, track, follow your intuition
* Throw finesse weapons and shoot missile weapons.
* See through illusions and deceptions, spot subtle influences and manipulations, resist confusion and disorientation, spot danger.

Charisma
* Inspire, intimidate, command, forge rapport, charm, negotiate, carouse, exert willpower, show discipline
* Resist fear, resist attempts to overwhelm or afflict your will and spirit, brush off social attacks and embarrassments
* Cast divine and faerie magic

## Combat

#### Attack Bonus

* Stat bonus (max +4) + class bonus (if any).
	* Forceful (including forceful thrown): +STR (+1 if DEX >= +1)
	* Finesse (including finesse thrown): +DEX (+1 if STR >= +1)
	* Missile (including finesse thrown): +WIS (+1 if DEX >= +1)
* If multiple options apply (e.g. Missile or Finesse for Finesse Thrown weapon or Forceful or Finesse for a Forceful/Finesse Melee weapon), use the best bonus.

## Magic

Max Casting Tier
* You cannot normally gain or cast spells of Tier > LVL.

Casting Bonus
* Stat bonus portion of casting bonus max +4; class bonus is added after.
	* Arcane: INT + 1 if WIS | CHA >= +1
	* Holy: CHA + 1 if WIS >= +1
	* Faerie: CHA + 1 if INT | WIS | CON >= +1

## Miscellaneous
---
### Backgrounds

* You are Trained in performing the various facets of your background(s).

### Size

* Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge, Gargantuan, Colossal
	* Each step above / below Medium doubles / halves the amount of food and drink needed each day, and increases / decreases the die rolled for falling damage 1 step.

### Encumbrance

Item State
* Readied
	* Readied items only need a minor action to access.
	* Should include weapons, shield, ammo, potions, scrolls, wands, etc you want to use.
* Worn
	* Armor, clothing, and gear such as helms, gloves, boots, etc are Worn.
	* Worn items can be accessed for the abilities / benefits.
* Stowed
	* Non-Readied items are Stowed and require action(s) to unearth in a hurry.
* Packed
	* Packed items are are so tightly packed and bound up to minimize space that they take a minute to pack away or pull out.

Item Size
* Coin: 500 coin per slot.
* Petty: negligible, except in great quantity.
	* Most gems, statuettes, jewelry, gloves, trinkets, circlets
* Small: fits in Small free item slots.
	* Wands, 1 scroll, 1 potion
* Bundle: 3x, 10x, 20x per slot.  Treat a half-full (or less) bundle (rounding down) as Small.
	* Potions (3x), Tokens / Scrolls (3x)
* Normal: 1 slot.
	* Idols, pots of spices, books, helms, crowns, jewelry boxes, boots, cloaks
* Bulky: 2+ slots.
	* Bolts of silk, rolled tapestries, paintings, furnishings
* Bodies: 10 + their STR
	* Unconscious / injured companions, corpses, captives

Capacity
* Personal
	* 3 slots (Readied).
	* 3 Small slots on belt and/or bandolier (Readied).
	* Up to 100 coins in a pouch (Readied).
	* Any reasonable number of Petty items (Stowed)
	* Garb, armor, wearables (Worn).
* Baggage
	* To have these slots, you must have a backpack or similar container(s).
	* 10 + STR | CON slots (Stowed).
	* 6 slots of Treasure (Packed)
	* If using any of these slots, you have a Medium Load.
* Burden
	* To access these slots you must have a backpacks, sacks, rope, etc.  All Stowed items (in any slots) become Packed.
	* 6 + STR | CON slots (Packed).
	* If using any of these slots, you have a Heavy Load.
	* If you exceed these slots, you are Overburdened.

Load
* Light Load
	* +A to outrun / out-maneuver those w/ heavier loads or of Large or larger Size.
* Medium Load (requires a backpack or similar container(s) to carry this much)
	* +D to swim and sheer climb.
* Heavy Load (requires a backpack and likely additional rope, sacks, etc)
	* All Stowed items become Packed.
	* Can't swim or sheer climb, +D to stat checks, attacks, resists, and casting, but not defense.
* Overburdened
	* Greater than a Heavy Load.
	* Can only stagger slowly under the load.  Can't take most actions, and +D to any you can.  Vulnerable to all physical damage and effects.  Automatically fail STR and DEX resist checks.

### Luck & Inspiration

* Luck
	* During an action, you can spend a Luck to reroll a die (d20 or damage), keeping whichever result you prefer.
	* Luck can be spent on behalf of allies.
* Gaining Luck
	* The GM can award Luck for various achievements: heroism, self-sacrifice, brilliant plans, moments of awesome, etc.
	* When you roll a natural 1 and suffer a critical failure, gain a Luck.
	* After a Long Rest, if you have >1 Luck, reduce to 1.
	* When you finish a Respite, if you have 0 Luck, gain 1.
* Inspiration
	* Inspiration is granted by spells, bardic inspiration, etc, and is spent like Luck.
	* You can only have 1 Inspiration at a time, and it is lost after a Long Rest.
